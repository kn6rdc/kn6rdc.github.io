Welcome

Welcome to my corner of the amateur radio world.

I'm an amateur radio operator interested in QRP, CW, portable operation, simple antennas, and the enjoyment that comes from making contacts with modest equipment.

This site is a place to keep notes about my station, operating experiences, equipment, antennas, and the things I've learned along the way.

Why QRP?

There's something satisfying about keeping things simple. A small radio, a modest antenna, a battery, a key, and a few watts can be enough to communicate around the world.

I'm less interested in collecting equipment than I am in actually using it.
