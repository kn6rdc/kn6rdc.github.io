This is a small little corner of the amateur radio world. There are others like it. But this one is mine.

I've been on the air circa 2021 and am primarily interested in QRP CW, SOTA, POTA, simple antennas, and the general enjoyment that comes from making contacts with modest equipment.

This site is just a place to keep notes about my station, operating experiences, equipment, antennas, and the things I've learned along the way.

Why QRP?

I've always enjoyed doing things with less than most people think is necessary—whether that's mountain biking, off-roading, hiking, or participating in SOTA. A few words come to mind: frugality, resourcefulness, and sufficiency. I enjoy finding ways to make things work with what I have.

I especially enjoy the reactions. Like passing a group of Jeeps on a backcountry trail, all modded out with every accessory imaginable, while driving a bone-stock Subaru. Or getting an email from another operator surprised that we made a contact with 5 watts and an EFRW antenna.

That's what I enjoy about the philosophy of QRP. As someone famous once said, "Do what you can, with what you have, where you are."

I'd add one more thing: enjoy it.
