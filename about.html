About

I'm an amateur radio operator interested in QRP, CW, portable operation, and simple stations.

This website is simply a place to document my experiences with amateur radio and keep track of the equipment, antennas, ideas, and lessons that have interested me along the way.

Amateur Radio

What I enjoy most about amateur radio is that it can be as complicated or as simple as you want to make it.

For me, the attraction is increasingly toward the simpler side: a small radio, a modest antenna, a key, a battery, and a quiet place to operate.

About This Site

This is an independent personal website. There are no advertisements, no commercial purpose, and no particular goal beyond sharing things I find interesting.

If something here is useful to another operator, that's a bonus.
