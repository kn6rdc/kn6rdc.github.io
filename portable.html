Portable Operation

One of the things I enjoy most about QRP is being able to take the station outside. A small radio, a battery, a simple antenna, and a key are enough to turn almost any quiet place into a radio station.

Portable operation also reinforces what I like about keeping things simple. There is only so much equipment worth carrying, so every item has to earn its place.

Getting Outside

Portable radio gives me a reason to combine amateur radio with hiking and exploring. The radio doesn't have to be the entire purpose of the outing. Sometimes it's simply something to enjoy along the way.

Portable Station

My portable station is centered around lightweight equipment and a small battery. I'll add more information here about the radio, key, battery, mast, antenna, and other equipment I actually use in the field.

Antennas

Portable antennas don't have to be complicated. Getting a wire into the air and finding a reasonably quiet place to operate can be more important than building an elaborate antenna system.
